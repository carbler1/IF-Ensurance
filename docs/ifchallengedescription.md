# If Insurance Could Do More

How can insurance become useful and relevant during important life events – and give a reason to choose and stay with If.

## Description

At If, insurance is often most visible when something goes wrong or when people face major changes in their lives. But events such as moving to a new home, buying a car, getting married or making other important life changes create needs long before there is a claim.

We want to explore how If could become genuinely useful during these moments – not only by providing insurance, but by helping people navigate the situation itself. At the same time, the solution should create credible business value for If through stronger customer relationships, retention, acquisition, risk reduction, greater efficiency or another clearly explained benefit.

Could If provide a solution that:

- Prevents something from going wrong
- Simplifies a difficult life change
- Guides customers towards better decisions
- Or offers something else that would provide value for customers and, in turn, for If

**Build a prototype of a product, service, experience, or concept that supports people through a meaningful life event—and shows why they would choose, use, and stay with If.**

## Insight

This is not simply a "build another insurance app" challenge.

The difficult part is identifying a real human need where an insurance company can provide meaningful value without making insurance itself the centre of the experience. The best idea might prevent something from going wrong, make a life change easier, help someone make a better decision, connect useful services together, or create something completely different.

AI, data and automation can absolutely be part of the solution, but technology is not the goal. The goal is something people would genuinely value — with a believable reason why it would also make sense for If as a business.

## Judging criteria

1. **Customer value and relevance**
   Does the solution address a real and clearly defined human need connected to a meaningful life event? Is the value to the customer easy to understand?
2. **Business value for If**
   Is there a credible mechanism through which the solution could benefit If — for example through customer retention, acquisition, risk prevention, reduced claims, efficiency or new value creation?
3. **Feasibility and scalability**
   Could the idea realistically be implemented and scaled? Does the team demonstrate how the required technology, data, partners or capabilities could work in practice?
4. **Innovation and experience**
   Does the solution challenge the traditional role of insurance and offer something more compelling than a generic insurance application or incremental improvement?

## Resources

Teams are encouraged to use public and open data that can help bring their concept to life. Possible resources include:

- Publicly available insurance terms and conditions from If and other insurance companies.
- Open data sources such as weather and forecasting data.
- Relevant public APIs, statistics and datasets.
- Other external data sources identified by the team.

Customer-specific or confidential insurance data will not be required for the challenge.
