How the service works

One loop runs for each risk in the home: find it, fix it, prove it, reward it, keep it fixed.

  1. 1Intake. Address lookup, photos, a questionnaire and documents describe the home. Unverified.
  2. 2Risk engine. An AI model turns the inputs into a short risk list. Each risk says why, and what fixing it is worth.
  3. 3Partner bidding. Vetted partners bid for the job (kilpailutus). The customer chooses.
  4. 4Verification. The partner confirms the finished job. An expert inspects when that is not enough.
  5. 5Mitigation score. One number. A breakdown shows what raised it and by how much.
  6. 6Rewards. Lower premium and deductible, partner discounts, tier benefits, checkups.
  7. 7Maintenance and alerts. The list updates itself from part age, partner reports, weather, season and new laws.
  8. 8Identity and delegation. Strong electronic ID. Family members act with delegated authorisation.
  9. 9If data console. Underwriters, claims handlers, sales and product teams, partner managers.
