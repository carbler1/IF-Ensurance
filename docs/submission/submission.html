If Life Partner

An app that helps people take care of their home before something goes wrong. And gives them a reason to stay with If while they do it.

Antton · Ege · Rasi

The moment

An older home comes with a report that says “much to repair”.

The owner does not know:

  • what matters most,
  • whom to call,
  • what waiting will cost.

Insurance meets the customer too late

A fire from an old fuse box. A water damage from old pipes. Today that is the moment the customer meets If.

We want If to be there before.

2
The idea

From a report to a reward, in six steps.

1
UploadThe customer uploads an inspection report (kuntotarkastus).
2
ReadA model reads the facts. Code decides the risks.
3
SeeUp to 5 risks, most serious first, with proof from the report.
4
BookOne tap books an If-vetted partner.
5
ConfirmThe partner confirms the work. Only outside proof counts.
6
RewardThe care score rises. Tiers unlock rewards.

Demo: a 68-year-old owner in Turku books an electrician for an old fuse box. The score goes 486 → 546 and unlocks Silver.

Criterion 3 · Feasibility and scalability

Code decides. Models read and write.

ReportA standard Finnish inspection report (KH 90-00394)
→
Model readsExtracts facts with source, quote and confidence
→
Code matchesFacts to risk catalogue. Severity, points and partner come from the catalogue only.
→
Model writesPlain customer text from catalogue hints
→
Code picksPartners and the maintenance list

Safe

A model cannot change a risk, a score or a price. It can only select catalogue ids. A person can review every text.

Private and cheap

The model runs on local hardware. Customer documents stay in the system.

How it scales

  • More risks: one catalogue entry each.
  • More things to care for: a car, a marriage, a new child. A new catalogue and score.
  • More partners: 14 types defined, with the proof each one gives.
  • More languages: plain text translates well.

Before launch: calibrate with If claim data, onboard partners, add strong e-ID, check premium rules.

Criterion 4 · Innovation and experience

If helps with the work itself: what to fix, who does it, what it gives back.

Proof, not promises

The score moves only when an outside source confirms a fix. The customer cannot fake it.

Terms as a to-do list

Sweep the chimney. Test the alarms. Close the main valve. Rules few customers read become tasks.

Traceable to the customer’s own words

Every risk shows the quote and the page. Unknown facts stay marked as unknown.

What comes next

A working house service first. Then everything people care for.

Phase 1 · now

The house: upload, risks, partners, maintenance list, score.

Phase 2

Full functionality, partner network, a view for If staff.

Phase 3

Finnish and Swedish. Then the car, and life events such as marriage or a new child.

Honest scope

Partners, prices, score numbers, tiers and discounts are mock data. The risk values are first estimates. The mechanism is real: read the customer’s document, match it to a catalogue, and reward outside-confirmed fixes.
