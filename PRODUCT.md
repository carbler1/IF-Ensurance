# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Explainer visuals: static HTML/CSS, one page per visual, each exported to PNG. The product itself is a native app; its stack is not decided.

## Users
- Primary product user: the average Finnish home owner, including older people. They want to avoid damage to their home without tracking every risk themselves.
- A family member may act for the customer through delegated authorisation, as in Suomi.fi and Kela.
- If staff: underwriters, claims handlers, sales and product teams, partner managers.
- Audience of the explainer visuals: If representatives at JunctionX Turku 2026, viewing them on the team's laptop.

## Product Purpose
If Life Partner finds the risks in a customer's home and life, explains how to reduce each one, and rewards the customer when an outside source confirms the fix. Success means fewer accidents and claims, and a reason for customers to choose and stay with If.

## Positioning
Insurance is not the centre of the experience. Prevention is. Rewards follow verified fixes, never self-reported claims. One combined mitigation score shows what raised it and by how much.

## Operating Context
- Finland first. Languages: Finnish, Swedish, English.
- Home first; car and personal life events later.
- Partners are vetted and listed by If, bid per job (kilpailutus), pay a listing fee and give discounts. They gain "certified by If" status.
- Full plan: `docs/iflifepartner.md`.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Inputs: address lookup, photos, questionnaire (unverified), document upload (trust level undecided).
- Proof of a fix: partner confirmation, or an expert inspection when that is not enough.
- Rewards: lower premium, lower deductible (omavastuu), partner discounts, tier benefits, free or discounted checkups.
- No data leaves If through the service. Any later sharing is If's decision and needs consent.
- Undecided: score tiers, decay rate, the "good enough" score threshold, legality of the pricing mechanism.

## Brand Commitments
The concept is presented to If (If Vahinkovakuutus Oyj). If's public brand is a blue round "if..." mark. The team owns no If logo asset and does not reproduce it.

## Evidence on Hand
- Challenge brief: `ifchallengedescription.pdf`.
- No user research, pilot data, partner agreements or figures exist. Visuals must not invent numbers, customers or quotes.

## Product Principles
1. Do not overwhelm the customer. Show only what matters.
2. Reward verified prevention, not promises.
3. Make If part of daily life without making insurance the centre.
4. Every party gains: customer, If and partner.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Older and less digital users are a core audience. Text must be large and plain.
